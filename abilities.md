# Proposed Quartz-Rank Abilities

This document outlines the two proposed starting (`Quartz`-rank) abilities for each of the eight Serpent Spire attunements, updated to reflect the correct mana types. These abilities are derived from the themes and examples presented in `attunement.md`. All stats are placeholders.
